SMS Consent & Opt-In Policy

Innovative Repairs

Last updated: November 2025

Innovative Repairs uses SMS text messaging to send customers appointment-related information and service updates. This page explains how we collect consent, what types of messages we send, and how you can opt out.

How We Collect Consent

Customers provide verbal consent during a phone call with our automated AI agent or a team member. During the call, the agent clearly asks:

“Do I have your consent to send a text message from Innovative Repairs with your appointment link to this phone number? Please say yes or no.”

Consent is only recorded when the customer responds with an affirmative answer (for example, “Yes”). Calls may be recorded for quality and compliance verification.

What Messages You Will Receive

You may receive text messages related strictly to service, including:

  • Appointment scheduling links (such as Calendly)
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Technician arrival updates or rescheduling notices
  • Follow-up messages regarding a completed service

No promotional or marketing texts are sent.

How to Opt Out

Reply at any time with the word:

  • STOP – to stop receiving messages

Other supported commands include:

  • HELP – for help information
  • START – to resume messages after opting out

Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Privacy & Data Use

Your phone number is used only for scheduling, service coordination, and customer support related to Innovative Repairs. We never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.
